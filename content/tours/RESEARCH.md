# Competitor research per tour

What the top Google results for each tour's main search have, and what was added
to our page. Our texts are written from scratch; competitors are used only to
see which information buyers expect.

## Private transfer Marrakech → Merzouga (53d59f0efda73e5dad4bc972)
Search: "private transfer Marrakech to Merzouga". Pages read: marrakechtaxi (transfer page),
noor-elite-services, daytrip.com, birdmi.
What they have: key-facts bar (560 km, ~7–8 h driving, 9–10 h with stops, price per vehicle,
group sizes); hour-by-hour route timeline; vehicle sizes; included / not included;
private vs shared tour vs bus comparison; airport pick-up option; departure-time advice
(leave 6–7 am, arrive for the sunset camel ride); tips (warm layer for the pass, book camps
early in peak season); luggage and child seats; reviews; 8–9 FAQs (time, price, route,
stopping at Ait Ben Haddou, airport, languages, Merzouga vs Erg Chebbi, return / onward to Fes).
Added: new overview with all of the above (no prices: they depend on group size),
richer day text, 13 FAQs; in 10 languages.

## 5-day Morocco desert tour for first-timers (7468e4edffd820c004dc080e)
Search: "5 days Morocco desert tour from Marrakech itinerary". Pages read: kimkim (5-day itinerary),
gowithguide, moroccoinsightexcursions (onthegotours blocked by bot protection, skipped).
What they have: narrative days naming specific places (Taddert argan cooperative, Amridil kasbah,
Khamlia, Rissani, Moulay Ali Cherif mausoleum), km and hours per day, room and vehicle types,
dietary requests, luggage left in the vehicle for the camp night, cancellation terms.
Added: "first-timer" angle (what to expect each day, money / tipping / photo / dress tips),
day-by-day km table, why the route works, rich days with schedule + tips + included,
13 FAQs (cold nights, camp comfort, tipping amounts, skipping the camel, cash, airport start,
kids, best season); in 10 languages.

## 3 days Marrakech → Merzouga → Marrakech (680bc6a5aa6f43072c7700f6)
Search: "3 day desert tour Marrakech to Merzouga itinerary camp". Pages read: local-morocco-tours,
TourRadar listing (operator page with traveller reviews and questions).
What they have: same Dades / Todra / Erg Chebbi route, 9 am departure, airport pick-up, shared vs
private tents. Traveller reviews and questions showed real worries: hot camp nights in summer (no AC),
unwanted shopping stops, arriving the day before, quad biking, camel ride length.
Our English was already rich. Added 4 FAQs answering those worries (arrive day before / airport,
no compulsory shopping, summer heat at camp, quad and sandboard), linked the Fes-ending FAQ.
Translations were short summaries with 8 FAQs: replaced with full translations in 9 languages.
