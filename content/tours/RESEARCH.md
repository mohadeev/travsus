# Competitor research per tour

What the top Google results for each tour's main search have, and what was added
to our page. Our texts are written from scratch; competitors are used only to
see which information buyers expect.

## Private transfer Marrakech → Merzouga (53d59f0efda73e5dad4bc972)
Search: "private transfer Marrakech to Merzouga". Pages read: marrakechtaxi (transfer page),
noor-elite-services, daytrip.com, birdmi.
What they have: key-facts bar (560 km, ~7–8 h driving, 9–10 h with stops, price per vehicle,
group sizes); hour-by-hour route timeline; vehicle sizes; included / not included;
private vs shared tour vs bus comparison; airport pick-up option; departure-time advice
(leave 6–7 am, arrive for the sunset camel ride); tips (warm layer for the pass, book camps
early in peak season); luggage and child seats; reviews; 8–9 FAQs (time, price, route,
stopping at Ait Ben Haddou, airport, languages, Merzouga vs Erg Chebbi, return / onward to Fes).
Added: new overview with all of the above (no prices: they depend on group size),
richer day text, 13 FAQs; in 10 languages.
